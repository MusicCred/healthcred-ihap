Find Help Where You Are

Whether you're inside, just released, or supporting someone coming home — these resources cover healthcare, housing, jobs, legal aid, and recovery support across Florida, Georgia, Alabama, and the nation.

Florida

IHAP's primary operating state — active across FL counties, 54-county presence

🏥 Healthcare Enrollment
Healthcare

ACA Enrollment Assistance

Free ACA marketplace enrollment for justice-involved individuals. Agents available for facilities and recently released residents across FL.

healthcred.com →
Healthcare

Florida DCF — Medicaid Application

Apply for Florida Medicaid online through the Department of Children and Families ACCESS portal. Available immediately upon release.

MyFlorida ACCESS →
Healthcare

Florida Community Health Centers

Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs) across FL provide sliding-scale care regardless of insurance status. No one turned away.

Available Nationwide

National Reentry Resources

These programs operate in all 50 states and can provide immediate help regardless of where you are.

🏛️

Reentry.gov

The federal government's official reentry resource hub. State-by-state guide to benefits, housing, employment, and healthcare after incarceration.

reentry.gov →
💊

Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program

Free HIV care and medications for uninsured individuals, including those recently released. No prior enrollment required in most states.

hab.hrsa.gov →
🤝

National Reentry Resource Center

Training, technical assistance, and resources for reentry practitioners, navigators, and justice-involved individuals across all 50 states.

nationalreentryresourcecenter.org →
⚖️

Reentry Council — ABA

The American Bar Association's reentry resource guide covers collateral consequences of conviction in every state — know your rights.

americanbar.org/reentry →
🧠

Mental Health America

Free online mental health screening, crisis resources, and state-by-state directory of mental health services accessible to formerly incarcerated individuals.

mhanational.org →

Children of Incarcerated Parents

cssp.org →

National Alliance to End Homelessness

Rapid rehousing and homelessness prevention resources specifically for justice-involved individuals. State and county finder available online.

endhomelessness.org →

Dave's Killer Bread Foundation

dkbfoundation.org →
