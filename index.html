Our Mission

Coverage is step one. We built what comes after.

The Inmate Health Access Program (IHAP) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit partnering with HealthCred inside correctional facilities across Florida, Georgia, and Alabama. We connect justice-involved individuals to their healthcare coverage, insurance guidance, and the full ecosystem of resources they need — before they leave custody and after they walk out.

IHAP works at three levels: training the coordinators and resource officers inside facilities so they can effectively get inmates signed up and prepared; connecting every person to their coverage and insurance information through the transition; and building an inmate-owned profile and wallet — a personal digital hub that connects each individual to every resource they need to succeed on the outside. Every year, 7 million Americans cycle through the justice system. Your gift helps make sure they don't cycle back.

How It Works

Train. Connect. Follow through.

IHAP is embedded alongside HealthCred inside correctional facilities — working with facility staff, inmates, and outside resources so every person has what they need before the gate opens.

1
Train Facility Staff
IHAP trains the coordinators and resource officers already inside the jail — the people who run the programs — so they know exactly how to get inmates enrolled, connected, and prepared before release.
2
Connect to Coverage & Guidance
Working with HealthCred, we ensure every inmate can access their health coverage, insurance information, and benefits guidance while still inside — not scrambling for it after they're out.
3
Coverage Activates at Release
Plans are structured to be active the moment someone walks out. A doctor, prescriptions, and mental health support available from day one — zero gap, zero waiting period.
4
Profile & Wallet Follows Them Home
The roadmap IHAP is building: every person leaves with their own digital profile and wallet — their coverage info, benefits, and connections to every outside resource they need, in one place.
