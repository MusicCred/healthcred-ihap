There's a Role for Everyone

Sheriffs. Nonprofits. Employers. Families. Faith communities. Whoever you are, IHAP has a way for you to make a lasting difference in the lives of people coming home.

Ecosystem Partners

Who Can Partner with IHAP?

IHAP works with seven distinct partner categories — each playing a unique role in the reentry ecosystem.

⭐

Sheriffs & Correctional Administrators

You're the first line. When facilities partner with IHAP, incarcerated individuals get access to healthcare navigation before they walk out the door — reducing emergency returns and lowering county costs.

  • Dedicated IHAP navigator embedded in your facility
  • Zero cost to the facility — fully grant and donor funded
  • Documentation support for county cost savings
  • Direct line to HealthCred enrollment infrastructure
Partner with IHAP
🤝

Reentry Programs & Inside Volunteers

Chaplains, counselors, jail ministry volunteers, and in-facility programs are already trusted by incarcerated individuals. IHAP training equips you to add healthcare navigation to your existing work — no additional staffing required.

  • Train-the-trainer certification program
  • Simplified enrollment guides and intake tools
  • Warm handoff referral network at release
  • Co-branded materials for your program
Get Trained
🌿

Community Navigators

Reentry navigators, case managers, and social workers are the bridge between the facility and the community. IHAP connects you with the tools, referral pathways, and coordination support to close the gap after release.

  • Referral portal access and case tracking
  • Healthcare enrollment coordination tools
  • Warm handoff from facility-based navigators
  • Training on Medicaid, ACA, and marketplace plans
Join the Network
🏠

Community Nonprofits & Housing Providers

Housing is the first step. Healthcare is the second. When your clients have coverage, they can access mental health care, addiction treatment, and medications — the supports that keep them housed and stable.

  • Referral partnership with IHAP navigators
  • Coordinated intake for housing + healthcare needs
  • Access to IHAP resource directory for your clients
  • Co-application for joint grant funding
Become a Partner
💼

Fair Chance Employers

Hiring justice-involved individuals is good policy and good business. IHAP supports your workforce by ensuring new hires have healthcare coverage from day one — reducing absences, improving retention, and demonstrating your commitment to second chances.

  • Healthcare onboarding support for new hires
  • Tax credit guidance for fair chance hiring
  • Recognition in IHAP employer directory
  • Workforce reentry consulting
Hire with IHAP
❤️

Individuals & Families

If someone you love is incarcerated or recently released, IHAP can help. Whether it's understanding their healthcare options, finding a navigator near their facility, or connecting them to community supports — you don't have to figure it out alone.

  • Family support and resource guide
  • Navigator referrals by county and state
  • Plain-language guide to healthcare at release
  • Peer support network connection
Find Resources
🏛️

Associations & Advocacy Organizations

Policy changes at the state and federal level are what make sustainable reentry possible. IHAP partners with sheriffs' associations, correctional health organizations, and advocacy groups to advance legislation that saves lives and cuts costs.

  • Coalition membership and co-advocacy
  • Policy brief contributions and data sharing
  • Joint legislative testimony support
  • Shared research and outcome tracking
