Privacy Policy

Inmate Health Access Program (IHAP) · Last updated: April 2026

IHAP — the Inmate Health Access Program — is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 41-4339110) based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our donors, volunteers, and site visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you donate, contact us, or use this website, we may collect:

  • Contact information: First and last name, email address, phone number
  • Mailing address: Collected when you request a donor perk (challenge coin shipment)
  • Payment information: Processed securely by Stripe. IHAP does not store credit card numbers, bank account information, or payment credentials of any kind.
  • Organization information: Company name, title, and EIN — collected optionally for corporate or foundation gifts
  • Usage data: Standard web analytics such as pages visited, browser type, and general location — collected anonymously

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Process your donation and deliver a tax receipt
  • Ship any donor perks (e.g., IHAP challenge coin) to the address you provide
  • Respond to your inquiries or requests
  • Send program updates or fundraising communications — only if you have opted in or donated
  • Maintain records required by the IRS and applicable law as a 501(c)(3) public charity

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Payment Processing

All online donations are processed through Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Your payment information is transmitted directly to Stripe over an encrypted connection and is never stored on IHAP's servers. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention

We retain donor records for a minimum of seven (7) years as required by IRS regulations for 501(c)(3) organizations. Mailing addresses collected for perk fulfillment are deleted within 90 days of shipment. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see Section 7).

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses minimal browser storage (localStorage) solely to remember your in-progress donation amount if you navigate away from the page. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We may use anonymous aggregate analytics to understand how visitors use the site.

6. Security

This website is served over HTTPS with SSL encryption. All donation transactions are processed through Stripe's secure, PCI-compliant infrastructure. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to our legal retention obligations)
  • Opt out of any future communications from IHAP

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@ihapusa.org or write to us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
